# Client-winning portfolio refinement

## Goal

Turn the existing portfolio into a clearer freelance sales experience that quickly shows what Deepak offers, proves relevant experience, and makes starting a conversation easy—without changing the established visual identity.

## Changes

- Rewrite the opening around client needs: professional WordPress sites, online stores, redesigns, speed improvements, and ongoing support.
- Add direct Email and WhatsApp actions in the opening, navigation, and final contact area.
- Add a concise Services section with clear offers and outcomes.
- Upgrade Selected Work with live website previews where the public sites allow capture, concise project context, and explicit “Visit website” links.
- Reframe WordPress expertise, process, experience, and toolkit around planning, communication, responsive delivery, testing, handover, maintenance, and measurable business value.
- Add a “Why work with me” section supported only by existing evidence, including current project experience and stated performance/user metrics.
- Add a client FAQ covering suitable projects, remote work, communication, WordPress handover, and ongoing support.
- Strengthen the final enquiry invitation while keeping email, phone, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp as direct links.

## Design and content safeguards

- Preserve the one-page structure, Space Grotesk/Manrope/IBM Plex Mono typography, charcoal/off-white/lime/coral palette, sharp geometry, marquee animation, and mobile menu.
- Do not invent testimonials, client results, pricing, availability guarantees, or unsupported claims.
- Keep the portrait and all factual details already present in the uploaded portfolio.
- If a project preview cannot be captured reliably, use the existing polished text treatment rather than a placeholder.

## Verification

- Check desktop and mobile layouts for readable text, stable spacing, and no overlap.
- Test navigation, menu behavior, project links, email, phone, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp destinations.
- Confirm accessible labels, keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, and complete page metadata.
